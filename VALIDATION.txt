- Per-clip dialogue, speaker, acting instruction and seed are recorded in sources/combat-voice-direction.json; output hashes and durations are recorded in dist/audio/enemy/manifest.json. Model weights are development-only and are not committed or served.
- Added actual damage-triggered pain reactions and urgent grenade/pain interruption while maintaining a single active speech channel, short urgent cooldowns, stable character voices and pause/mute/death stopping.
- 2.25 vehicle traversal and grass implementation files are unchanged. Large original startup pack names remain unchanged.
- Independent local Whisper-small and SenseVoice-int8 transcription audits are used to identify unclear words. This checks approximate word agreement; it does not establish acting quality or naturalness. No subjective listening pass is claimed.
- Full rendering in shared Chrome remains blocked by the browser's disabled WebGL context. Real iPhone rendering, driving and subjective listening have not been checked in this session.

Final V2.26 results:
- Static asset/module checks: 408 local files, 22 3D models, 53 JavaScript modules and 126 module references; all passed.
- 9 cactus/grass/voice resource and playback regressions, 6 new combat voice scheduling checks and 70 full game regressions passed. CI also retains the arm/grip, vehicle traversal and water checks.
- 30 MP3s independently decoded by FFmpeg; 447912 bytes total; duration range 0.864 to 3.504 seconds. Mean signal levels are within -40 to -8 dBFS and maximum decoded sample peak is -2.2 dBFS.
- Final Whisper-small and SenseVoice-int8 audits each agree phonetically with 29/30 clips. Their single disagreements concern different clips; every clip agrees with at least one recognizer. Optional erhua and introductory pain sounds are handled explicitly. Exact transcripts, hashes, levels and disagreements are preserved in sources/combat-voice-audit.json.
- Shared Chrome decoded all 30 final clips with its actual AudioContext in running state. Actual GameAudio playback returned 0.81s for scout alert, 1.53s for veteran attack and 2.33s for leader search. The actual EnemyChatter -> GameAudio path switched spot -> grenade and retained exactly one active speech source. This is audio-component validation, not a rendered gameplay or subjective listening claim.
- Temporary preview configuration and the audio QA page were removed before publication.
